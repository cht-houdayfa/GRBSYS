Our services

Our areas of expertise

Scope

01

Consulting & advisory

  • Project owner support (AMO)

    Consulting and technical support to scope and steer your technology projects according to good practice and applicable frameworks.

  • Knowledge transfer

    Ongoing training programmes for your engineers and technical managers so they can manage modern technologies independently.

Build

02

Digitalisation & document management

  • Process re-engineering

    Turning your paper-based and manual procedures into automated digital workflows so you can work faster, in line with applicable regulations.

  • Online portals & platforms

    Unified online platforms to receive, process and track requests from your customers and partners: forms, online requests, customer portals.

  • Electronic document management (EDM)

    Secure archiving of your organisation’s records, with indexing and fast search to find documents easily and reduce the risk of loss or damage.

03

Systems integration & APIs

  • Enterprise application integration

    Configuring and securing APIs for reliable data exchange between your platforms, partners and internal systems.

  • Data-flow encryption

    Encryption of data exchanged between your systems and your partners, with strict control over authorised access.

04

AI & data

  • Automatic document classification (NLP)

    Natural Language Processing (NLP) to automatically read, analyse and classify your correspondence and documents.

  • AI chatbots

    Generative-AI chatbots on your platforms to guide your users and answer common questions in natural language, around the clock.

  • Dashboards & predictive analytics

    Dynamic business intelligence (BI) dashboards to analyse your indicators, even with large data volumes, and anticipate trends for data-driven decisions.

Secure & run

05

Cybersecurity & compliance

  • Vulnerability assessment & penetration testing (VAPT)

    Controlled attack simulations (ethical hacking), carried out with your written authorisation and within an agreed scope, to identify vulnerabilities in your systems and networks and help you fix them before they are exploited.

  • Incident response

    Automated response procedures and rapid intervention to contain attacks and limit their impact on your services.

  • Regulatory support & security frameworks

    Support in bringing your information systems in line with the requirements that apply to you (DGSSI directives and frameworks, personal data protection) and recognised good practice: gap assessment, action plan, implementation and audit readiness.

06

Cloud, infrastructure & DevOps

  • Cloud migration

    Migrating your systems and databases from your current environment to a secure cloud environment, limiting service interruption as far as possible.

  • Server hardening & network security

    Hardening your servers, deploying web application firewalls (WAF) and managing secure virtual networks.

  • Encrypted backup & disaster recovery

    Automated, encrypted off-site backups and disaster recovery plans (DRP) to restore your data within agreed timeframes after a major incident.

  • Go-live & environment management

    Managing per-environment configuration and domain names for a smooth, secure move from development and testing to production.

07

Technical assistance & managed services

  • Reinforcing your teams with technical expertise

    Our engineers and experts carry out technical assistance assignments agreed with you (scope, deliverables, duration), under GrbSys's own supervision, on your premises or remotely.

  • Managed IT services (outsourcing)

    Technical and administrative oversight of your IT systems, including the administration of your servers, through annual management and maintenance contracts.

  • On-site & remote support

    Our support teams work on your premises or remotely, with coverage up to 24/7 depending on the agreed service level (SLA).
